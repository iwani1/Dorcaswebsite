Returns

Can I return or exchange something?

Within 7 days of delivery, yes — unworn and in its original packaging. We will exchange or refund, your choice. Earrings cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrive damaged. Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned unless faulty.

What if it arrives damaged or faulty?

Send us a photograph the same day and we will replace it or refund you in full, including delivery. See Returns & Refunds for the detail.

The jewellery

Will the gold finish wear off?

Gold-plated pieces are plated, not solid. Put them on last and take them off first — after perfume and lotion, before the shower — and they will hold their finish for years. Brass develops a patina that many people prefer; sterling silver tarnishes naturally and comes back with a silver cloth.

How do I know my ring size?

Measuring a ring you already own is more accurate than measuring your finger. Our Size Guide has the table in millimetres. If you are between sizes, size up.

Every piece arrives boxed and retail-ready, with its symbol and what it means. Gift sets come in one box. If you are sending it directly to someone, tell us and we will leave the price out.
