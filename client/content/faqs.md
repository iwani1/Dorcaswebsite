## Frequently asked questions

### Ordering

**How do I place an order?**
Choose a piece, add it to your cart and check out. You can pay by card (Visa,
Mastercard) or mobile money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo). Payment is
handled by Paystack, so your card details never touch this site.

**Do I need an account to order?**
No. You can check out as a guest. An account only exists so you can see your
orders again later.

**Can I pay on delivery?**
In **Accra only**, and by prior arrangement — write to **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** before
ordering and we will confirm. Outside Accra, payment is online at checkout.

**Can I order something that is sold out or made to order?**
Yes. Pieces marked *made to order* take **[NUMBER] working days**; we confirm the
date with you before we start. If a piece is sold out, email us and we will tell
you honestly when it is coming back.

### Delivery

**How long does delivery take?**
Accra: 1–2 working days by dispatch rider. Other regions: 2–4 working days by
courier. Full details and charges are on our **Shipping & Delivery** page.

**Do you deliver outside Ghana?**
Not yet. If you are abroad and want a piece, email us and we will tell you whether
we can get it to you and what it would cost — we would rather say no than take money
for something we cannot deliver.

### Returns

**Can I return or exchange something?**
Within **7 days** of delivery, yes — unworn and in its original packaging. We will
exchange or refund, your choice. **Earrings cannot be returned** for hygiene reasons
unless they arrive damaged. Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be
returned unless faulty.

**What if it arrives damaged or faulty?**
Send us a photograph the same day and we will replace it or refund you in full,
including delivery. See **Returns & Refunds** for the detail.

**When do I get my money back?**
Refunds go to the original payment method through Paystack, within **[NUMBER]
working days** of the piece reaching us. Your bank or mobile money provider may take
a few more days to show it.

### The jewellery

**Will the gold finish wear off?**
Gold-plated pieces are plated, not solid. Put them on last and take them off first —
after perfume and lotion, before the shower — and they will hold their finish for
years. Brass develops a patina that many people prefer; sterling silver tarnishes
naturally and comes back with a silver cloth.

**How do I know my ring size?**
Measuring a ring you already own is more accurate than measuring your finger. Our
**Size Guide** has the table in millimetres. If you are between sizes, size up.

**Do you make custom or engraved pieces?**
Sometimes, in small batches. Email **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** with what you have in mind,
your quantity and your date, and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it.

**What is the packaging like?**
Every piece arrives boxed and retail-ready, with its symbol and what it means. Gift
sets come in one box. If you are sending it directly to someone, tell us and we will
leave the price out.

### Wholesale

**Do you sell to shops?**
Yes. Minimum **6 pieces per style**, at **50% of the retail price**, boxed and
retail-ready. Apply on the **Wholesale** page and we will review your account within
one working day.

**Is my payment secure?**
Yes. Everything is served over HTTPS, and payment is processed by **Paystack**, a
provider licensed by the Bank of Ghana. We never see or store your card number,
CVV, or mobile money PIN.
