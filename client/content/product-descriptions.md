# Ten product descriptions

Paystack's wording is "a **complete** description of the goods sold". Right now the
products have a name, a price and — for nine of them — no photograph and no text.

Two fields per product:

- **Short description** — one line, shown next to the price and the add-to-cart
  button.
- **Description** — the body of the product page.

Paste them in **Products → [product] → Product short description / Product
description**. Everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` is a measurement or a material
only the client can confirm — the prose is finished, the facts are hers.

## The facts only the client can supply

Fill these once, then every description above is finished:

| What | Where it is needed |
|---|---|
| The metal of each piece | gold-plated brass? sterling silver? beaded? |
| The post metal on the four earrings | sterling silver or surgical steel — this is an allergy question, not a preference |
| Pendant and band measurements in mm | every description |
| Chain and cord lengths in cm | all five necklaces/bracelets |
| Weights in grams | all ten |
| The available ring sizes | the three ring products |
| Which five symbols are on QD-009 | one line |

**Do not describe plating as gold.** Gold-plated, gold-tone and solid gold are three
different claims, and the first one is the one that loses a chargeback when a customer
says the description was misleading.
