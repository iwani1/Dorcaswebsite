## Returns and Refunds

We want you to be happy with what arrives. If you are not, here is exactly what
we will do.

### The short version

- Tell us within **7 days** of delivery.
- The piece must be **unworn, unaltered, and in its original packaging**.
- We will **exchange it or refund it** — your choice.
- **Earrings cannot be returned** for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive damaged
  or faulty.
- **Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned** unless faulty.
- If we sent the wrong piece, or it arrived damaged, **we pay for the return** and
  put it right.

### What can be returned

Any unworn piece in its original packaging, within 7 days of delivery, except the
pieces listed under *What cannot be returned* below.

### What cannot be returned

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **Earrings** | Hygiene. This is standard for jewellery retailers and we cannot make exceptions. |
| **Made-to-order pieces** | They were made for you and cannot be resold. |
| **Personalised or engraved pieces** | Same reason. |
| **Gift cards** | Not exchangeable for cash. |

If any of these arrives **damaged or faulty**, that is a different matter — send us
a photograph the same day and we will replace it or refund you in full.

### The condition we need

- Unworn and unused — tried on indoors is fine, worn out of the house is not.
- No perfume, lotion, makeup or marks on the piece.
- In its original box or pouch, with any tags still attached.
- Packed so it cannot be crushed in transit.

We may decline a return that does not meet these conditions, or offer a partial
refund, and we will tell you why.

### How to start a return

1. Email **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** or call **[PHONE NUMBER]** within 7 days of delivery.
2. Tell us your **order number**, what you want to return, and whether you want an
   **exchange or a refund**.
3. We will reply within **two working days** with the return address and any
   instructions.
4. Send the piece back to us. **Please do not send anything before we reply** — we
   need to know it is coming.

### Who pays for the return

- **You changed your mind:** you pay the return delivery.
- **We sent the wrong piece, or it arrived damaged or faulty:** we pay it, and we
  will refund any delivery charge you paid on the original order.

### Refunds

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**, through **Paystack**.
- We process the refund within **[NUMBER] working days** of the piece reaching us
  and passing inspection.
- Your bank or mobile money provider may take a few more days to show it. That part
  is outside our control.
- We will email you to confirm the refund has been issued.
- **Delivery charges are refunded only where the item was faulty or incorrect.**
- **Wholesale orders** are governed by the separate wholesale agreement, not this
  page.

### Exchanges

If you want a different size or piece, tell us when you write. If the replacement
costs more, we will send you a payment link for the difference; if it costs less,
we refund the difference. If the piece you want is out of stock, we will tell you
honestly how long it will take, or refund you instead.

### Damaged or faulty on arrival

Send us a photograph **the same day**, showing the piece and the packaging. We will
replace it or refund it in full, including delivery, and we will arrange collection
at our cost. Faults caused by wear and tear, accidents, or contact with perfume,
lotion, water or chlorine are not manufacturing faults — see our care guidance.

### Nothing here reduces your rights

These terms sit alongside your rights under Ghanaian consumer law, not instead of
them. If you think we have got something wrong, tell us and we will look at it
again.
