## Shipping & Delivery

### Where we deliver

| Where | How it travels | How long | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accra** | Dispatch rider | 1–2 working days | **GH₵ [X]** |
| **Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale and other regional capitals** | Courier | 2–4 working days | **GH₵ [Y]** |
| **Elsewhere in Ghana** | Courier | 3–5 working days | **GH₵ [Z]** |

**Free delivery in Accra on orders over GH₵ [AMOUNT].**

Delivery charges are calculated at checkout before you pay. The price you see
there is the price you pay — there are no charges added after your order is placed.
If a delivery charge looks wrong for your area, contact us before ordering and we
will correct it.

### When we dispatch

Orders placed before **[TIME]** on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after that, or at the weekend, go out the next working day. We do not
dispatch on Sundays or public holidays.

### Made-to-order pieces

Pieces marked *made to order* take **[NUMBER] working days** to make before they
are dispatched. We confirm the date with you before we start. If a piece is needed
for a specific date, tell us when you order and we will say honestly whether we can
make it.

### Tracking

Every parcel is packed and tracked by us before it leaves. You will receive a
message with your tracking details once it is on its way. If tracking has not
updated for more than two working days, contact us and we will chase the courier
for you.

### Receiving your order

- Please give us a phone number that is answered during the day — most deliveries
  in Accra are arranged by phone.
- If nobody is available, the rider or courier will call and try again. A parcel
  that cannot be delivered after two attempts is returned to us; we will refund the
  item but not the delivery charge.
- **Check the parcel before you sign for it** where you can. If the packaging is
  damaged, tell the rider and photograph it.

### Lost, damaged or delayed

If a parcel is lost or damaged in transit, tell us within **48 hours** and we will
deal with the courier on your behalf, at our cost. Where the piece is unavailable
to replace, we will refund you in full.
