## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This policy explains what **Queen Dor's Fashion House** does with your personal
information when you use **queendorfashionhouse.com**, in line with the **Data
Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)** of Ghana.

### Who we are

**[REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME]**, trading as **Queen Dor's Fashion House**,
[ADDRESS], Accra, Ghana. Registration number **[NUMBER]**.

For anything to do with your data — including requests to see, correct or delete
it — write to **[EMAIL ADDRESS]**. We will respond within **30 days**.

### What we collect

| What | When |
|---|---|
| **Your name, delivery address, phone number, email address** | when you place an order, create an account, or contact us |
| **What you ordered and what you paid** | when you place an order |
| **Messages you send us** | when you use the contact form, email us or call us |
| **Your email address** | if you sign up for our newsletter |
| **Basic technical data** — pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type | automatically, through cookies and analytics |

We do **not** collect or store your **card number, expiry date, CVV, or mobile money
PIN**. Payment is handled entirely by **Paystack**, a payment service provider
licensed by the Bank of Ghana, on their own secure checkout. You can read
Paystack's privacy policy on their website.

### Why we use it

- **To deliver your order** — we cannot ship without a name, address and phone
  number.
- **To take payment** — through Paystack.
- **To answer you** — when you ask a question or report a problem.
- **To keep proper records** — invoices and sales records, as tax and accounting
  law requires.
- **To improve the site** — understanding which pages are used, in aggregate.
- **To tell you about new pieces** — only if you have asked us to. You can stop
  this at any time.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

### Who we share it with

| Who | What they get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Paystack** | your name, email, phone, order total | to process payment |
| **Our delivery partners** (dispatch riders, couriers) | your name, address, phone | they cannot deliver without them |
| **Our email and hosting providers** | whatever passes through them | to run the site and send your order emails |
| **Our accountants** | sales and invoice records | statutory accounting |

We share the minimum each of them needs for their own job. Some of these providers
may process data outside Ghana; where that happens, it is under a contract that
requires them to protect it to a standard equivalent to Act 843.

We will also disclose information where we are **legally required** to — for
example to a court, the Ghana Revenue Authority, or the Data Protection
Commission.

### How long we keep it

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Order and invoice records | **6 years** (tax and accounting requirement) |
| Account details | until you ask us to close the account |
| Contact messages | 2 years |
| Newsletter subscription | until you unsubscribe |
| Analytics data | [12 months] |

### Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask us to:

- **tell you** what personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** anything that is wrong or out of date;
- **delete** data we no longer need a lawful reason to keep;
- **stop** using your data for marketing;
- **object** to a particular use of your data.

Write to **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not
satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the **Data Protection Commission**
of Ghana.

### Cookies

We use cookies to keep your shopping cart working, to remember that you are logged
in, and to understand which pages people use (analytics). You can block or delete
cookies in your browser settings — but if you block the essential ones, the cart and
checkout will stop working.

### Security

The site is served over **HTTPS**, so the information you type is encrypted in
transit. Card and mobile money details are entered on Paystack's systems, not ours.
Access to order data is limited to the people who need it to fulfil orders. No
system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you
and the Data Protection Commission as required.

### Children

Our site is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect
their data. If a child has given us their details, contact us and we will remove
them.

### Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we
make a significant change to how we use your data, we will tell you by email before
it takes effect.
