We reply to emails within **one working day**, and usually much sooner. If you have
not heard from us in two working days, please call — it means the email did not
reach us.

### What to include in your message

So we can help in one reply rather than five, please include:

- your **order number**, if your question is about an order (it is in your
  confirmation email, and looks like `#1234`);
- the **email address or phone number** you used to order;
- a **photograph**, if something arrived damaged;
- the **date** you need a piece by, if it is a gift.

### Common questions first

- **Where is my order?** Check the tracking message we sent when it was dispatched.
  If tracking has not moved for two working days, write to us.
- **Can I return or exchange something?** Yes, within 7 days — see *Returns and
  Refunds* below for the exceptions.
- **Do you deliver outside Accra?** Yes, by courier, across Ghana.
- **Can I order for a shop or buy in bulk?** Yes — see *Wholesale* below.

### Order problems

If an order is wrong, damaged or late, email **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** with your order
number in the subject line and we will deal with it. Please do not open a payment
dispute before contacting us — most problems are a courier delay we can fix in a
day, and a dispute can take weeks.

### Wholesale and stockist enquiries

We supply boutiques and stockists across Ghana. Minimum order is **6 pieces per
style**, at **50% of the retail price**, boxed and retail-ready. Tell us your shop
name, where you are, and which pieces you are interested in, and we will send trade
terms. You can also read the *Wholesale* page first.

### Press, collaborations and custom work

We do take on custom and small-batch work. Email us with what you have in mind,
your quantity and your date, and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it.
