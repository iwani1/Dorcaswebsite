brass, sterling silver and beaded pieces — necklaces, earrings, bracelets, rings and
gift sets — sold **retail to individual customers** across Ghana and **wholesale to

### What we make

Every piece is built around an **Adinkra symbol**, and every symbol already means
something before we touch it.

- **Gye Nyame** — the supremacy of the divine; what holds everything together.
- **Sankofa** — go back and fetch it; what you carry with you.
- **Akoma** — the heart; patience and endurance.
- **Duafe** — the wooden comb; care, and the beauty of good grooming.
- **Dwennimmen** — the ram's horns; humility alongside strength.
- **Nkyinkyim** — the twisting; adaptability, changing with circumstance.
- **Adinkrahene** — the chief of symbols; leadership and greatness.
- **Bese Saka** — the sack of cola nuts; wealth through unity.

We did not invent these meanings and we do not need to. We make them wearable.

**Materials:** gold-plated brass, solid brass, sterling silver, and hand-beaded
pieces. Each product page states what that piece is made of, its size and its
weight. Our jewellery is **hand-finished in small batches**, so small differences in
finish and dimension between pieces are normal and are not defects.

### How it is made

Pieces are designed in Accra and hand-finished here, in small runs — cast or
assembled, filed, polished and plated before each one is boxed. Made-to-order pieces
are made after you order them, which is why they take longer and cannot be returned.

### Retail and wholesale

- **Retail:** order directly from this site. Prices are in Ghana cedis, and payment
  is by card or mobile money through **Paystack**.
- **Wholesale:** we supply boutiques and stockists. Minimum **6 pieces per style**,
  at **50% of the retail price**, boxed and retail-ready. Trade accounts are approved
  individually — see the *Wholesale* page.

### Delivery and returns

We deliver across Ghana: central Accra by dispatch rider in 1–2 working days, other
regions by courier in 2–4. Returns are accepted within 7 days, with the usual
exception of earrings for hygiene reasons. Full details are on the *Shipping &
Delivery* and *Returns and Refunds* pages — those pages form part of our terms.
