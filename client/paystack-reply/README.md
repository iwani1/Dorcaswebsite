## Why she is asking

The list is not arbitrary. Paystack's Merchant Services Agreement requires a merchant
to publish its official name, a description of the goods, a returns and refund policy,
contact details with an email or phone number, a physical address, a delivery policy
and a data protection policy. An activation request is where they check that the
website and the business registration describe the same business.

The two additions she names that the site did not have are **FAQs that aren't lorem
ipsum** and a **cancellation policy**. Both are in this folder, written.

## On "proof of purchase / supplier invoice"

She is asking how the stock was acquired. For a business that makes its own pieces,
that means invoices for the components — the gold-plated findings, chains, boxes,
packaging — not a single purchase of finished goods. Send whichever exist, dated, in
the business name. If a component is bought from a market trader with no invoice, say
so plainly and send what you do have; an honest partial answer is better than a
silence on the point.
