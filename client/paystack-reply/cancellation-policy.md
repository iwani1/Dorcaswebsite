# Cancellation policy — the clause Paystack is missing

Her email says "**refund and cancellation policy**". The site has returns and
refunds; it has no cancellation clause. That is a gap a reviewer will notice,
because "I changed my mind before it shipped" is the commonest request a shop gets.

Paste §1 into `/returns-exchanges/` near the top, and §2 into `/terms/` as a short
addition to section 7.

---

## 1. Cancelling an order — paste into the Returns page

### Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order at any time **before it is dispatched** and we will refund
you in full, including any delivery charge.

**How to cancel:** email **[EMAIL ADDRESS]** or call **[PHONE NUMBER]** with your
order number, as soon as you know. If the order has not yet left us, the
cancellation is immediate.

**If it has already been dispatched,** you can refuse the parcel when it arrives, or
accept it and return it under the returns policy. Refusing a parcel means the courier
brings it back to us; we refund the item once it reaches us, but not the delivery
charge.

**Made-to-order and personalised pieces** can be cancelled at any time before we
begin making them. Once work has started, they cannot be cancelled or returned
unless faulty — we will always confirm with you before we start, which is your last
chance to cancel.

**Refunds for cancellations** go to the original payment method through Paystack,
within **[NUMBER] working days**, and we will email you to confirm.

**If we cancel your order** — because a piece is out of stock, the price was listed
in error, or we cannot deliver to your address — we refund you in full, including
delivery, and we will tell you why.

Cancelling an order does not affect any other right you have under Ghanaian consumer
law.

## 2. Reference in the Terms — paste into section 7

Add this to the end of §7, *Returns, exchanges and refunds*:

> **Cancellation.** You may cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched and
> receive a full refund, including delivery. Made-to-order and personalised pieces
> may be cancelled at any time before we begin making them. Where we cancel your
> order, we refund you in full. The full cancellation policy forms part of these
> terms and is published on our Returns page.

---

## Why this matters beyond the form

Two practical reasons, not just compliance:

1. **A cancellation clause is what stops a dispute becoming a chargeback.** A
   customer who cannot find a cancellation route calls their bank instead.
2. **It sets the made-to-order boundary in writing.** Those pieces are the only ones
   where "we already started" is a legitimate answer, and it has to be said before
   the order, not after.
