## Returns and exchanges

**Earrings cannot be returned** for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive damaged or faulty. This is standard for all jewellery retailers and we cannot make exceptions.

Everything else:

- Contact us within **7 days** of delivery
- The piece must be unworn, unaltered, and in its original packaging
- We will exchange it or refund it — your choice
- **Made-to-order and personalised pieces cannot be returned** unless faulty

If something arrives damaged, send us a photo on WhatsApp the same day and we will replace it at our cost.

Return delivery is paid by the customer unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong piece.

## Care

**Gold-plated** — the plating is a finish, not the metal. Put your jewellery on last and take it off first: after perfume, after lotion, before the shower. Wipe with a soft dry cloth and store in its pouch, separately from other pieces.

**Brass** — develops a natural patina with wear, which many people prefer. Polish with a soft dry cloth to bring back the shine. Keep it away from water, perfume and chlorine.

**Sterling silver** — tarnishes naturally in air. Wearing it slows tarnishing; a silver cloth brings it back. Store sealed, not loose in a drawer.

**Beaded** — keep away from water, perfume and hair products. Store flat, not hanging.

## Privacy policy

Queen Dor's Fashion House ("we") sells jewellery online to customers in Ghana.

**What we collect:** your name, delivery address, phone number, email address, and the details of what you ordered. If you pay by card or mobile money, the payment is handled by our payment provider — we never see or store your card details.

**Why we collect it:** to deliver your order, to contact you about it, and to keep the records the law requires us to keep. If you tick the newsletter box, we also use your email to tell you about new pieces — you can unsubscribe at any time.

**Who we share it with:** our delivery partners (your name, address and phone number, because they need them to deliver), our payment provider, and our email provider. Nobody else. We do not sell your data.

**How long we keep it:** order records for **six years**, as required for tax and accounting. Marketing consent until you withdraw it.

**Your rights:** under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to **___@___** and we will respond within 30 days.

**Cookies:** the site uses cookies to keep your cart working and to understand which pages people use. You can block them in your browser, though the cart will stop working.
